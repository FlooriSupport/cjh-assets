# Attendee prep — send this Wednesday

Double duty: it genuinely helps people finish, and it filters out the people who
were never going to show. Someone who won't set up a $20 account on Wednesday was
not going to build anything on Friday.

Send as its own email/text Wednesday, and link it from the Luma page.

---

```
Friday, 1–4 PM. Here's what to do before you show up so you don't lose build time.

TAKES FIVE MINUTES, DO IT TONIGHT

1. A paid ChatGPT Plus or Claude Pro account. $20. The free tiers cut you off
   around minute forty and you'll spend the back half of the session watching a
   rate limit instead of building. This is the one non-negotiable.

2. Know your logins. Whatever we're automating — your CRM, your email, your phone
   system, your Google Business Profile — make sure you can actually get into it.
   Bring your password manager. Every single time I run one of these, somebody
   loses an hour to a password reset. Don't be that person.

3. If your build touches your phone system, check whether you can get to the
   settings yourself or whether your vendor controls it. If it's your vendor,
   email them today.

BRING

- A laptop. Not an iPad. You cannot build on an iPad.
- Your charger.
- Real data. Pick whichever applies:
    · a customer or lead list, exported to CSV
    · your last 5 proposals or quotes
    · 5 real replies you've sent to inbound leads (we'll teach the AI your voice)
    · a P&L export
  Real data is the difference between a demo and a thing you'll actually use.

- One number: what a new customer is worth to you. Average job, or lifetime value.
  You'll use it to decide what's worth building.

THINK ABOUT ONE THING

What's the thing you do every week that you shouldn't be doing?

Not the big strategic thing. The small annoying repetitive thing. The follow-up you
forget. The proposal that eats your Saturday. The calls you miss at 4:45. The list
you keep meaning to build.

That's your build. Come with it in your head and you'll be building by 1:35 instead
of still deciding at 2:15.

LOGISTICS

Doors 12:40, and I'd take that — we start at 1:00 sharp and the first thing that
happens is me building something in front of you. Worth being in a seat for it.

At 3:25 everybody demos what they made. 90 seconds. Yes, everybody. That's the deal.

Address and parking come Thursday afternoon.

Questions before Friday, text me: (469) 277-9385

— Yousef Okasheh
```
