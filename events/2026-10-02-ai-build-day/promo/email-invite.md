# Email invite

Plain text. No header image, no template, no buttons. It should look like you typed
it, because a designed email reads as marketing and marketing gets archived.

## Subject lines — pick one, A/B if your tool allows

1. `Friday, 1pm, North Dallas — you leave with it built`
2. `20 seats, free, Friday: build one AI thing that actually runs`
3. `no slides, no panel, no pitch — Friday afternoon`
4. `are you free Friday afternoon?`  ← highest open rate, lowest polish. Use for warm lists only.

## Body

```
[First],

Short one.

Friday, October 2. 1 to 4 PM. North Dallas. In person. Free. 20 seats.

It's an AI build session for business owners, and the only thing that makes it different from every other AI event in Dallas is that you leave with something working.

The format:

At 1:00 I build one live in front of you from scratch, ten minutes, no slides, so you can see exactly what the bar is. At 1:15 the room votes on the three builds we're doing. At 1:30 you write yours down in one sentence — trigger, steps, output. Then we build for two blocks. By 3:25 yours runs end to end even if it's ugly, and you demo it to the room in 90 seconds.

That demo is the whole design. Nobody finishes what they don't have to show.

What's on the menu — we vote, top three win:

  1. A map of every decision maker in your market: name, title, company,
     work email, direct mobile. Yours to keep.
  2. Missed call, text back in 30 seconds.
  3. Inbound lead, real personalized reply in 60 seconds, booked to your calendar.
  4. Notes in, finished branded proposal out.
  5. Monday 7am brief: who in your market just hired, moved, expanded or raised money.
  6. Every Google review answered in your voice, queued for approval.
  7. Voice note after a site visit, CRM note and follow-up email written for you.
  8. Your numbers in, plain-English answer to what's actually making you money.

You don't need to be technical. You need a laptop, admin access to your own tools, and a paid ChatGPT or Claude account. If you can log into your CRM and your email, you can build what we're building. The people who struggle are never the non-technical ones — they're the ones who can't get into their own systems.

And if you've got someone who handles your marketing or your tech, bring them. This works better with two from the same business: one who knows what's broken, one who knows where the logins are.

Also bring real data — a customer list, your last five proposals, or your actual inbox.

It's free. The price is that you finish something and demo it. There are no observers — 20 seats, 20 people building.

Seat's here: [LUMA LINK]

I'm approving registrations by hand, and there's a question asking what you want built by 4 PM Friday. Answer it properly and you're in.

Exact address goes out Thursday.

Yousef Okasheh
Commercial Job Hunters
(469) 277-9385
commercialjobhunter.com
```

## Sending notes

- **From your personal address, not a marketing platform**, if the list is under ~300.
  Deliverability and reply rate are both dramatically better.
- **Over 300, or a cold-ish list:** use your sending platform, but keep it plain text.
- **Send Tuesday 7:00 AM CT.** Resend to non-openers Thursday 6:30 AM with subject
  `re: Friday` and one line above the original: "Bumping this — down to [X] seats."
- **Reply-to must be you**, and actually watch it. Half your registrations will come
  from replies, not the link.
