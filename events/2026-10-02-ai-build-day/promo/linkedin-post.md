## Small things that matter

- **Reply to every comment within the first hour.** LinkedIn weights early engagement
  heavily, and this post will get "what tools?" comments you can answer in one line.
- **Answer "can I join remotely?" with a clean no.** "In person only — the demos at the
  end are the whole point and they don't work over Zoom." That no makes the event
  more attractive, not less.
- **DM everyone who comments or reacts.** Not a blast — a real message: "Saw you
  reacted. Want me to hold you a seat?" That's the conversion, not the post.
- **Don't edit the post after the first hour.** Adding "link in comments" is fine; a
  substantive edit resets distribution.
