# Text messages — your highest-converting channel. Treat it accordingly.

You have a phone and relationships. That beats every algorithm in this document.
Realistic expectation: a personal text to a warm contact converts **10–30x** better
than a Facebook event post.

## Rules that keep this working

1. **Send from GoHighLevel / LeadConnector if that number is A2P 10DLC registered**
   (you already run LeadConnector — check the registration status before you blast).
   If it isn't registered, carriers will filter the messages and you'll never know.
2. **If you're sending from your personal phone, batch it.** 20–25 at a time, spaced
   out over the day. A personal number firing 200 identical messages in ten minutes
   gets flagged as spam by the carrier, not by the recipients.
3. **First name every single one.** No "Hey there."
4. **No link shorteners.** Not bit.ly, not tinyurl. Carriers filter shortened links
   hard. The raw `lu.ma/...` link is clean and trusted — use it as-is.
5. **One link per message, max.**
6. **Don't send the same message to a group thread.** Individual sends only.

## B · The "I picked you specifically" version — use for your best 20
Highest conversion of anything here. Worth the extra 30 seconds each.

```
[First] — doing a free 3hr AI build session Friday afternoon in North Dallas. Everybody picks one thing and actually builds it before they leave.

Was thinking about [SPECIFIC THING ABOUT THEIR BUSINESS — the missed calls, the proposals taking all weekend, the follow-up that never happens]. That's exactly the kind of thing we'd knock out.

20 seats, free. Want me to hold you one?
```

Note that last line. **"Want me to hold you one?"** gets a reply. A link gets a
scroll-past. Then you send the link to the people who say yes — and those people show up.

## D · Follow-up to non-responders
Wednesday. Do not resend the pitch.

```
[First] — seats going for Friday's AI build thing. Down to [X]. Yes or no is fine, just want to know whether to hold one.
```

Short, asks a closed question, gives them an easy out. This outperforms a second pitch.

```

[VENUE NAME]
[FULL ADDRESS]
```

(8pm tonight), and the waitlist (social proof + loss). Anyone who doesn't reply Y
probably wasn't coming, and now you know Thursday instead of finding out Friday.
