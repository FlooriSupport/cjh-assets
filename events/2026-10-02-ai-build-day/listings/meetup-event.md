# Meetup — my recommendation: skip it for Friday. Here's why, and here's the copy anyway.

## The honest math

You asked whether to do Meetup. For **this** event, no:

1. **It costs money.** Hosting on Meetup requires an Organizer subscription. You'd be
   paying a monthly fee for one event you're giving away free.
2. **A brand-new group has no reach.** Meetup's distribution comes from existing group
   members and from its topic-based recommendations, both of which key off group
   history. A group created Monday for an event on Friday is effectively invisible.
3. **The lead time is wrong.** Meetup audiences plan 1–3 weeks out. Four days is
   below the floor for that crowd.

You would spend two hours Monday setting up a group, and get maybe one or two RSVPs
from people who don't know you.

## When Meetup becomes the right call

If this works and you make it **monthly**, Meetup turns into your single best
recurring-attendance channel in DFW — better than LinkedIn for this, honestly, because
Meetup members actively browse for exactly this. The group compounds; the algorithm
starts feeding you people who attend other AI and business events in Dallas.

**So: run Friday's event on Luma. If the room is good, create the Meetup group next
week and list November's session with three weeks of runway.** That's the sequence
that actually works.

## Faster alternative for this week, if you want more local reach

Post in existing DFW Meetup groups instead of creating your own. Find Dallas AI,
Dallas entrepreneur, and DFW small-business groups, and message the organizer directly:

> Hey [name] — I'm running a free hands-on AI build session for business owners
> Friday afternoon in North Dallas. Not a pitch, everyone leaves with something
> working. 20 seats. Would it be okay to share it with the group, or would you rather
> I send it to you and you decide? Happy to do it either way. — Chris

Organizers say yes to that framing far more often than to a link drop.
