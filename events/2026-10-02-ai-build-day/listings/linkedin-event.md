# LinkedIn Event — copy/paste

**Create at:** linkedin.com → Home → "Events" in the left rail → Create event
**Event type:** In person
**Registration:** Choose **"Use an external registration link"** → paste your Luma URL.
Trade-off, so you know what you're giving up: LinkedIn's native RSVP form gives you
attendee names + profiles and a bit more feed distribution. External linking gives you
ONE list instead of two. With 4 days to run this, one list wins — two lists means
somebody doesn't get the address on Thursday.

Friday, October 2 · 1:00–4:00 PM CT · North Dallas
Free · 20 seats · Bring a laptop

This is not a webinar, a panel, or a "future of AI" talk.

You show up with a real problem in your business. Three hours later you leave with a working thing that runs without you. That is the entire event.

HOW IT WORKS

1:00 — I build one from scratch in front of you, start to finish, in ten minutes. No slides. You see exactly where the bar is.
1:15 — The room votes. We pick the three builds the most people want. You join the one you need.
1:30 — You write your build in one sentence: trigger, steps, output. This is the part everyone skips, and it is why most people's AI projects die in a browser tab.
1:35 — Build block one.
2:35 — Build block two. Hard rule: by 3:25 it runs end to end, even if it is ugly.
3:25 — Everybody demos. Ninety seconds each. You run your thing in front of the room.
3:50 — What breaks first, what to do Monday, how to hand it to your team.

THE MENU — we vote, top three win

01 · Decision-maker map. Every named buyer in your market, with title, company, work email and direct mobile, in a spreadsheet you own and keep.
02 · Missed call, text back in thirty seconds.
03 · Inbound lead, a real personalized reply in sixty seconds, booked straight to your calendar.
04 · Notes in, finished branded proposal out.
05 · Monday 7am opportunity brief. Who in your market just hired, moved, expanded or raised money, and the exact line to open with.
06 · Every Google review answered in your voice, queued for your approval.
07 · Talk into your phone after a site visit, get back the CRM note, the follow-up email and the next task.
08 · Dump your numbers in, get a plain-English answer to "what is actually making me money."

WHO THIS IS FOR

Owners and operators of real businesses doing real revenue. Contractors, commercial services, home services, agencies, B2B shops. If you have customers, a phone that rings, and something you keep meaning to automate, you are who I built this for.

WHO THIS IS NOT FOR

Anyone who wants to watch. There are no observers in this room. Twenty seats, twenty people building.

A laptop. Not an iPad.
Your charger.

Real data. A customer CSV, your last five proposals, your actual inbox.
One number: what a new customer is worth to you.

THE PRICE

It is free. The price is that you finish something and demo it. If you are not going to build, please leave the seat for someone who will.

I build decision-maker maps for commercial contractors — 5,955 mapped buyers across Dallas–Fort Worth at last count, with verified work emails and direct mobiles. I am not an AI guy who wandered into business. I am a business guy who got tired of buying lists that were wrong, built the thing myself, and now does it for a living. Everything I am showing you Friday is something I actually run inside my own company.

## After you publish
1. Click **Invite** and invite connections — LinkedIn lets you invite in bulk.
   Filter by location: Dallas–Fort Worth. Do NOT blast all connections; LinkedIn
   throttles invites and DFW-only keeps the attendee list real.
2. Post the announcement post (`promo/linkedin-post.md`) **separately** and attach
   the event to it. The post is what actually travels; the event page just holds.
3. Comment on your own post with the Luma link about an hour after posting.
   LinkedIn suppresses posts with outbound links in the body.
