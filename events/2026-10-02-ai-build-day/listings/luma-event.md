## Registration questions — this is the whole show-rate strategy

You gave up price as a filter, so commitment has to come from somewhere else.
Make these **required**:

1. **What business do you own or run?** (short text, required)
2. **What do you want built by 4pm Friday?** (long text, required)
   Nobody writing a paragraph about their business ghosts you. This single question
   is worth more than a $25 ticket.
3. **Which build is closest to yours?** (multi-select, required — list all 8 from the menu)
   Doubles as your pre-vote. You walk in Friday already knowing which three tables to set up.
4. **Mobile number for the Thursday confirmation text** (required)
   Say plainly: "I text the address Thursday. No list, no marketing, just this event."

Then approve registrations manually. Approve anyone who answered #2 with a real
sentence. Anyone who wrote "idk" or "AI" gets a reply asking what they want built —
half will answer and become your best attendees.

```
### 🔴 LIVE AND IN PERSON — Friday, October 2
**1:00–4:00 PM CT · North Dallas, TX**
Free · 20 seats · Bring a laptop

This is not a webinar, a panel, or a talk about the future of AI.

You show up with a real problem in your business. Three hours later you leave with a working thing that runs without you. That is the entire event.

### How it works

**1:00** — I build one from scratch in front of you, start to finish, in ten minutes. No slides. You see exactly where the bar is.
**1:15** — The room votes. We pick the three builds the most people want. You join the one you need.
**1:30** — You write your build in one sentence: trigger, steps, output. This is the part everyone skips, and it is why most people's AI projects die in a browser tab.
**1:35** — Build block one.
**2:35** — Build block two. Hard rule: by 3:25 it runs end to end, even if it is ugly.
**3:25** — Everybody demos. Ninety seconds each. You run your thing in front of the room.
**3:50** — What breaks first, what to do Monday, how to hand it to your team.

### The menu — we vote, top three win

**01 · Decision-maker map.** Every named buyer in your market: title, company, work email, direct mobile. In a spreadsheet you own and keep.
**02 · Missed call → text back in thirty seconds.**
**03 · Inbound lead → a real, personalized reply in sixty seconds,** booked straight to your calendar.
**04 · Notes in → finished, branded proposal out.**
**05 · Monday 7am opportunity brief.** Who in your market just hired, moved, expanded or raised money — and the exact line to open with.
**06 · Every Google review answered in your voice,** queued for your approval.
**07 · Talk into your phone after a site visit** → CRM note, follow-up email, and next task, written for you.
**08 · Dump your numbers in** → a plain-English answer to "what is actually making me money."

### You don't need to be technical

This is the part people get wrong about events like this. You are not writing code and you are not learning a programming language.

What you actually need is three things: **a laptop, admin access to your own tools, and a paid ChatGPT or Claude account.** That's it. If you can log into your CRM and your email, you can build what we're building.

The people who struggle are never the non-technical ones — they're the ones who can't get into their own systems. So if your web guy set up your forms, or your phone system is controlled by a vendor, sort that out before Friday.

And if you have someone who handles your marketing or your tech, **bring them.** This works better with two people from the same business: one who knows what's broken, one who knows where the logins are.

### Who this is for

Owners and operators of real businesses doing real revenue. Contractors, commercial services, home services, agencies, B2B shops. If you have customers, a phone that rings, and something you keep meaning to automate, you are who I built this for.

### Who this is not for

Anyone who wants to watch. There are no observers in this room. Twenty seats, twenty people building.

### What to bring

- A laptop. Not an iPad.
- Your charger.
- A paid ChatGPT or Claude account. The free tiers will stall you around minute forty and you will spend the back half watching a rate limit.
- Logins for whatever your build touches — CRM, email, phone system. **Bring your password manager.** People lose entire sessions to password resets.
- Real data. A customer CSV, your last five proposals, your actual inbox.
- One number: what a new customer is worth to you.

### The price

It's free. The price is that you finish something and demo it. If you're not going to build, leave the seat for someone who will.
```
