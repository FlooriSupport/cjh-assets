almost no reach and can't be boosted. Page events can.

## Where Facebook actually pays off this week
The event page itself will do almost nothing in 4 days. The groups will.
Post the event link + the first two paragraphs into local groups Monday night
and again Wednesday. Realistic targets:

- DFW / Dallas small business owner groups
- Plano, Frisco, Richardson, Addison, McKinney community + business groups
- Local contractor, roofing, HVAC, plumbing, landscaping trade groups
- DFW networking groups (BNI-adjacent, chamber-adjacent, referral groups)
- Dallas startup / tech / AI groups — lower ICP fit, higher click rate

**Read each group's rules first.** Most allow promo only on a specific day, or only
in a pinned thread. Getting banned Monday costs you the week.

**Frame it as a question, not a flyer.** Posts that open with "Any DFW owners actually
using AI for something real, or is everyone still just messing with ChatGPT?" and then
mention the event in the third line outperform a straight promo by a wide margin.
